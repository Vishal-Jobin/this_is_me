Design and photography, read together.

Practice

Your Name trained as a graphic designer and picked up a camera as a way of thinking through the same problems — composition, contrast, restraint — outside a brief. The two have never really separated since.

Design work here tends to start from structure: a grid, a type system, a set of rules a project can live inside. Photography starts from the opposite direction — from noticing something in the world first, and only understanding its structure afterwards. Working in both keeps each one honest.

Recent projects span visual identity, editorial design and art direction for independent studios, labels and publications, alongside an ongoing personal photography archive shown on this site.
